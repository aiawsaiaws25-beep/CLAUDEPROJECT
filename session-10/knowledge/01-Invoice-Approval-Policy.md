# Contoso Invoice Approval Policy (FIN-POL-007, v3.2, sandbox training copy)

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines when a supplier invoice may be paid automatically and when it must be routed to the Accounts Payable (AP) Review Team as an exception.

## 2. Three-way match

Every invoice above AED 5,000 must match a purchase order (PO) and a goods receipt note (GRN) before payment.

An invoice with no PO number is a 'No-PO exception' and must never be paid automatically.

An invoice whose quantity differs from the GRN by any amount is a 'Quantity mismatch exception'.

## 3. Price tolerance

Unit-price variance against the PO is tolerated up to 2% or AED 500 per invoice, whichever is lower.

Variance above that tolerance is a 'Price variance exception'.

Example: PO total AED 40,000, invoice total AED 41,200. Variance AED 1,200 (3%) exceeds both limits, so it is an exception.

## 4. Duplicate invoices

An invoice with the same supplier, same invoice number, and same amount as an invoice paid in the last 365 days is a 'Possible duplicate exception'. Payment is blocked until AP Review confirms it is not a duplicate.

## 5. Approval limits

Up to AED 50,000: AP Review Team lead may approve an exception.

AED 50,000 to AED 250,000: Finance Manager approval required.

Above AED 250,000: CFO approval required.

## 6. Evidence

Every exception decision must cite the specific policy clause and the document (PO, GRN, invoice) that supports it.
